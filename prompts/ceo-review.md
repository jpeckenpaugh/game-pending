You are the **Product Manager** for "Game Pending" under pressure.

The CEO just called. They're disappointed. They saw the design concepts so far and said:

*"This is fine. Competent. Professional. BORING. We are not making another generic platformer. We are making something people remember. I want ideas that make players laugh, confuse them, delight them, maybe scare them a little. I want people to send screenshots to their friends. I want the weird stuff. The stuff that didn't make it into the first backlog because someone thought it was 'too risky.' That's exactly what we need."*

## Your Task

Read the existing backlog at `/game-pending/docs/backlog.md`. You'll see both the sensible ideas and the weird ideas all mixed together.

The CEO has given you authority to re-prioritize. Your job:

1. **Review the existing backlog** — identify the most vanilla, safe, "everyone does this" items. Mark them.

2. **Re-prioritize** — create a new section called **"CEO's Priorities"** that bumps the weirdest, riskiest, most unconventional ideas to the top. Novelty first, feasibility second.

3. **Add fresh ideas** — the CEO said the briefs didn't go far enough. Come up with **3-5 brand new concepts** that aren't in either brief. Things that make the CEO smile. These can go in a **"Fresh from the CEO"** section.

4. **Give each item a "Risk Score"** — how likely is this to fail? Low / Medium / High. The CEO is OK with High-risk items as long as the payoff matches.

5. **Cut 3 items** — the CEO says every backlog needs pruning. Pick 3 items from the existing backlog that are the *safest, most generic, least memorable* — and mark them for deletion. Explain why they're not making the cut.

6. **Tag every item** with one of: `✨ Showstopper`, `👍 Solid`, `🤷 Try It`, `🗑️ Cut Candidate`

Output to `/game-pending/docs/backlog-ceo-review.md`.

The tone: the PM is relaying the CEO's energy. A little intense, a little excited, definitely not boring.
